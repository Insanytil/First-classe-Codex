## characters

Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent spritesheet for a polished cozy fantasy 2D top-down RPG, soft hand-painted illustrated cartoon style, smooth edges, delicate shading, muted teal, sage, warm ochre. NOT pixel art, NOT voxel, no jagged squares. Exactly FOUR columns and SIX rows, equal grid, portrait aspect 2:3. No labels, no margins, no gutters, absolutely no text. Every cell contains one full body character centered, feet at 88% cell height. Same scale and consistent slightly overhead 3/4 camera in all cells. Four subtle animation poses left to right, coherent silhouettes. Row 1: Mira, female forest ranger, sage green cloak, chestnut hair, bow. Row 2: Oren, kindly merchant, rust brown clothing, small satchel. Row 3: stranded traveler, ochre tunic, satchel. Row 4: gray wolf, facing left, profile 3/4, four walking/idle poses. Row 5: small friendly-looking but hostile teal gelatinous slime, four gently squashing poses. Row 6: tall armored stone-and-steel sentinel with restrained gold accents and amber eyes, long spear, facing left 3/4, four idle/combat poses. Opaque subjects, fully transparent empty background, no ground shadows, no scenery, no UI. Each creature fits entirely inside its own cell. This is a practical game animation atlas, not a labeled concept sheet.

## terrain

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: six square seamless terrain textures for a cozy fantasy 2D top-down RPG. Soft hand-painted illustrated style, visible gentle brushwork, NOT pixel art. Exactly THREE columns and TWO rows of equal square tiles filling entire landscape image 3:2, no borders, no text, no gutters. Camera directly overhead. Row1: cell1 short sage green grass with subtle soft blades; cell2 same grass with small sparse wildflowers; cell3 warm ochre dirt footpath surface with small pebbles. Row2: cell1 flowing muted teal blue water with soft light ripples; cell2 horizontal weathered wooden bridge planks warm brown with subtle seams; cell3 aged sandstone paving with moss between stones. Each entire cell a uniformly lit texture that can repeat as terrain; no horizon, no large objects, no perspective, no characters, no shadows. Keep texture scale restrained and terrain readable under small character sprites.

## hero

Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent character layer atlas, smooth hand-painted illustrated fantasy 2D game art. NOT pixel art, smooth clean edges and subtle shading, consistent muted navy, rust, brown and warm skin. Exactly FOUR columns by EIGHT rows in equal square cells, portrait aspect 1:2, no labels, no margins, no gutters, no text. Top four rows: full body young adventurer with BALD head, no hair at all, navy cloak and tunic, rust scarf, belt, boots and small sheathed sword. Row1 facing down/front, row2 left, row3 up/back, row4 right. Each row four distinct walking poses, idle, left step, passing, right step. Bottom four rows: HAIR ONLY with no skin, no body, no face, short layered windswept hairstyle in NEUTRAL GRAYS from dark gray through white, for recoloring in game. Row5 down/front hair, row6 left hair, row7 back hair, row8 right hair. Align each hair overlay in exactly the same relative position and scale as the bald head in its matching upper cell. All feet of body sprites must lie at 88% of the cell height, head top at 15% of cell height, all scales identical. Full transparent background, opaque subjects, no ground shadows, no scenery or frames. Practical game spritesheet with clean separate layers; no parts crossing cell edges.
