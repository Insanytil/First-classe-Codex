## ari

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production transparent JRPG character animation spritesheet.
Primary request: Create ARI, an original adult male warrior for a polished hand-painted fantasy Pygame RPG. Input images are STYLE AND ANIMATION REFERENCES ONLY: their mature painted fabric, leather, steel, coherent full-body anatomy and meaningful attack motion. Do not copy the reference face. Ari has short tousled dark chestnut hair, a focused face, navy sleeveless layered tunic, rust-red scarf, one worn brass shoulder guard, leather bracers and boots, practical steel longsword. Natural adult proportions, detailed illustrated game art, smooth clean edges, NOT chibi. Original character.
Composition: EXACT FOUR columns and SIX rows, 24 equal rectangular cells, regular invisible grid, no gutters. All characters same scale and centered in each cell; feet baseline at 92 percent cell height. Rows 1-4 walking animation: row1 FRONT/down, row2 LEFT, row3 BACK/up, row4 RIGHT. Each row four poses: idle, left step, passing, right step. Rows5-6 are EIGHT sequential sword attack frames, all facing LEFT (allied combat characters stand on the RIGHT): ready; crouched anticipation; sword raised; slash starting; full contact extended LEFT; follow-through; recovery; ready. Genuine changing arms and torso across attack frames. Full sword, feet and every limb entirely within each cell, ample margins. Consistent head size, identity, clothes and lighting across all 24 poses. Hair INCLUDED, weapons INCLUDED; no separate overlays for this new fixed character. All empty space truly transparent alpha. No text, numbers, labels, visible grid, background, floor, external shadows, scenery or effects. Deliver only the usable animation atlas, no concept layout.
